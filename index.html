Questions clinics ask

FAQ

Will it give wrong medical advice?

No. It only answers booking, price, hours and clinic-policy questions from information you approve. Anything clinical is answered with “please speak to the dentist” and the patient is offered an appointment or your phone number.

What if a patient needs a human?

The assistant offers your phone number at any point and can hand the conversation to your team during opening hours. Urgent pain cases are always pushed to call immediately.

Do I need to change my website or calendar?

No. It sits on your current website and works with Google Calendar and most practice diaries. If you have no online calendar yet, I’ll set a simple one up as part of the build.

How much does it cost?

Pricing is simple and quoted on the short call, based on your clinic size — a one-off setup plus a small monthly fee for hosting, calendar connection and support. No long contract is required to start, and it’s significantly less than agency quotes because you’re working directly with me.

Can it match our clinic’s name, prices and tone?

Yes — that’s the whole point. This demo uses a sample clinic (“BrightSmile”). Yours uses your name, logo colours, real treatment list, real prices and your way of speaking to patients.

Do we have to take both website and WhatsApp?

No — it’s set up the way your clinic wants: website only, WhatsApp only, or both together in one inbox, with the voice agent on or off. We decide it together on the 10-minute call.

How does the voice agent work?

Patients tap the call / microphone button and simply talk. The assistant listens, answers out loud and books the appointment by voice — ideal for older patients and anyone who dislikes typing. Try “Voice call” at the top of the demo above (best in Chrome or Edge).
